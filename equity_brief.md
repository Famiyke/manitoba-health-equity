# Manitoba Health Equity | Illustrative Regional Planning Brief

> **Portfolio simulation.** The RHA observations and index scores were generated for an analytical demonstration. They are not official Manitoba Health estimates and must not be used to rank actual regions.

## Decision question
How can a regional health planning team compare indicators while acknowledging ecological uncertainty?

## Work demonstrated
- Constructed example regional indicators for five Manitoba RHAs.
- Designed comparison tables, an illustrative composite index and exploratory correlations.
- Produced Excel, Python and SQL reporting examples.

## Interpretation
The original example uses only five geographic observations. A correlation among five regions does not identify causes, establish individual-level relationships or support a 'strongest predictor' claim. Composite indices depend on scaling and chosen weights. Simulated access, mortality and prevalence rates must not be presented as measured conditions.

## What must be validated
1. Extract year-matched indicators from official provincial reporting sources.
2. Check definitions, denominators and population adjustment.
3. Publish index weights and sensitivity testing.
4. Avoid causal interpretations of ecological associations.
5. Include community and regional input before suggesting interventions.
