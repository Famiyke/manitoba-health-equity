# Source validation boundary

Manitoba Health Annual Statistics publication starting point: https://www.gov.mb.ca/health/annstats/

The project uses generated regional health authority indicators. Its reported Northern RHA access and mortality rates, composite scoring and five-region correlations are simulated and **cannot be treated as official provincial findings**.

A correlation across five ecological units does not establish causation or any individual-level determinant. An index ranking depends on normalization, chosen indicators and weights.

A legitimate source-based update requires documenting the exact Manitoba Health report and year for every metric, matching RHA boundary definitions and denominators, assessing adjustment and uncertainty, and documenting sensitivity to composite-index choices.

**Validation status:** Source catalogue identified; region-level source figures, index results and workbook outputs not independently reconciled.
